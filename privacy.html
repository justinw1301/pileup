PILE UP – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

PILE UP is a game made by Justin Wheatley ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

This version of PILE UP does not collect any personal data. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • A random player ID and tag (for example "Brave Otter 4821"). They are created on first launch and stored in your device's Keychain or app storage. They are not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or device identifiers, and they never leave your device.
  • Game progress and settings, such as your best distance, your own deaths on today's course, whether the sound is muted and whether you have seen the tutorial.

Deleting the app removes this data. The Keychain ID may remain until you reset your device.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or free-text input.
  • No advertising, analytics or third-party tracking software.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No location, contacts, photos or other personal data is accessed.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the game creates an image and some text about your run and opens your device's share sheet. Whatever you send, and where you send it, is up to you. That content goes to the app you choose, not to us.

Children

PILE UP does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds online features, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released. The effective date above will change.
