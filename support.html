How to play

Your runner sets off on its own. Tap to jump, and hold to jump higher. If you hit a hazard, your body stays where it fell and you restart straight away. Bodies are solid: they fill pits, cover spikes and build ramps up walls. Reach the flag to clear the day.

FAQ

When does the course change?
Every day at midnight UTC (00:00 GMT).
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No account is needed. The game also plays without a connection.
Does it cost anything or show ads?
No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
How do I turn the sound off?
Use the mute button in the game.
How do I reset my progress?
Delete and reinstall the app.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The game plays in landscape.
